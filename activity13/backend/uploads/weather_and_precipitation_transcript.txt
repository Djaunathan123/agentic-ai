Weather refers to the condition of the atmosphere at a
particular place and time. It includes temperature, humidity, wind,
cloud cover, and precipitation. Weather changes from day to day and
sometimes even from hour to hour.
00:35 
So, what exactly is precipitation?
Precipitation is any form of water that falls from clouds and reaches
the Earth’s surface. It happens when tiny water droplets or ice crystals
in clouds become too heavy to stay suspended in the air.
1:00 
There are several types of precipitation.
The first and most common is rain. Rain occurs when liquid water
droplets combine and become heavy enough to fall from clouds.
The second type is snow. Snow forms when temperatures are below
freezing, causing water vapor to turn directly into ice crystals.
1:30 
Another type is sleet. Sleet consists of small ice pellets that
form when raindrops freeze before reaching the ground.
There’s also hail, which forms during strong thunderstorms. Hailstones
grow as they are carried up and down by powerful winds inside storm
clouds before falling to the ground.
2:00 
Several factors influence precipitation. One important factor is
temperature. Warm air can hold more moisture than cold air. When warm,
moist air rises, it cools and condenses into clouds. If enough moisture
accumulates, precipitation occurs.
2:25 
Humidity also plays a major role. Higher humidity means there is
more water vapor in the air, increasing the chance of cloud formation
and rainfall.
Wind is another factor because it moves moist air from one region to
another. Mountains can also affect precipitation by forcing air to rise,
cool, and produce rain on one side while leaving the opposite side much
drier.
3:00 
Meteorologists use different instruments to measure weather
conditions. A rain gauge measures the amount of rainfall. A thermometer
measures temperature. A barometer measures air pressure, while an
anemometer measures wind speed.
3:30
Weather forecasts use information collected from satellites,
radar systems, weather stations, and computer models. These tools help
predict whether precipitation is likely and what type it will be.
4:00 
Understanding precipitation is important because it affects
agriculture, transportation, water supplies, and daily activities.
Farmers rely on rainfall for crops, while heavy precipitation can
sometimes cause flooding or landslides.
4:25
Before planning outdoor activities, it’s always a good idea to
check the weather forecast. Being prepared can help keep you safe during
storms or heavy rain.
